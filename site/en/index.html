No new crisis. But there is a war.

Online, there is no new crisis right now. But there is a war: Russia has been waging it against Ukraine since 2014. Officially, the Netherlands is not at war. It is involved, though, since MH17 at the latest. That is why the crisis level is 2.

The war goes on

Russia has been waging war against Ukraine since 2014. That war is not over. These clocks keep running until it is.

Since the occupation of Crimea20 February 2014The date set by Ukrainian law in 2015. Russia’s own medal “For the Return of Crimea” says 20 February too.

Since the war in Donbas12 April 2014An armed unit led by the Russian Igor Girkin seizes Sloviansk.

Since MH1717 July 2014A Russian Buk missile shoots down flight MH17 from Amsterdam to Kuala Lumpur over Donbas. All 298 people on board die, 196 of them Dutch. In 2022 a Dutch court convicts three men of murder and finds that Russia was in control of the fighters in Donbas.

Since the full-scale invasion24 February 2022Just before 05:00 Kyiv time, Putin announces a “special military operation”. Within minutes, the attack begins.

    PHP2Shell is closed. The patch is out, the sites are back and the worst is over. The whole crisis, with every update, stays at crisus.nl/php (in Dutch). What should you do?

Nothing special. That is what is special.

  1. Update. Any time, also when nothing is going on. Especially then.
  2. Make a backup. And restore one. A backup you never restored is a hope.
  3. A password manager and two-factor authentication. For example QtPass, with pass and GPG underneath.
  4. Turn on the hum. If it is too quiet for you.
